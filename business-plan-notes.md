# Business Plan Notes

## Core product definition

The uploaded plan describes a self-serve web app that converts up to 10 property photos into a branded walkthrough video, with an optional room-furnishing add-on for vacant units. It positions two customer motions on one product: self-serve subscriptions for individual agents, and contact-sales recurring retainers for developers and agencies. It emphasizes native Arabic UI, RTL support, GCC listing conventions, local-currency display, and bundled virtual staging as the primary differentiation.

## Go-to-market and audience

The plan targets individual agents, small agency teams, and larger agencies or developers. It proposes GCC-targeted paid social, an Arabic landing page, a free trial hook, and upsell from agent tier into higher tiers, with enterprise handled through contact sales.

## Pricing assumptions in the plan

The uploaded pricing table proposes: Free trial at $0 for one watermarked video; Pay-per-video at $19 for one video; Starter at $39/month for 4 videos; Growth at $99/month for 12 videos plus 3 furnish credits; Team/Agency at $249/month for 20 videos plus $9 per extra video; and Enterprise from $899/month with custom bundle pricing. The plan also notes a separate furnish add-on price of +$5/video for Starter/Pay-per-video, included allowance on Growth, and bundled furnishing on Team/Enterprise.

## Unit-economics assumptions in the plan

The plan's confirmed vendor-pricing table uses Kling 2.1 Standard for video generation at $0.056/second, GPT-4o mini for room classification, Flux Kontext Pro for furnishing/staging at $0.04/image, Stripe at 4.4% plus $0.30, Hetzner CPX31 worker VPS at about $25/month, and Supabase Pro at about $25/month. For a 10-image tour, it estimates video generation at $2.80, vision classification at $0.01, FFmpeg assembly at $0.08, QA retry buffer at $0.42, base COGS at $3.31, and all-in COGS with furnishing at $3.71.

## Financial target assumptions

The business plan targets roughly $875K ARR as the realistic level needed for a $3.5M exit at a 4x multiple. It models a blended gross margin of roughly 61%, about 885 paying accounts to reach target ARR, and a three-to-five-year time horizon to exit readiness.

## Operating and risk assumptions

The plan requires customer review and confirmation of AI room sequencing before generation, support for GCC-specific room types, and a mandatory disclosure on AI-furnished outputs. The risk table highlights competition, vendor pricing drift, AI misclassification, disclosure risk, unmeasured churn, and enterprise concentration as core execution risks.
