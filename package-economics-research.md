# Package Economics Research

## Currency conversion basis

The Central Bank of Bahrain states that the BHD is fixed against the USD at **1 BHD = 2.659 USD**. This fixed-rate basis is used to convert USD-denominated provider costs into BHD.

Source: https://www.cbb.gov.bh/facilities-interest-rates/

## Managed composition cost

Shotstack’s official pricing page lists pay-as-you-go video rendering at **$0.30 per rendered minute** with a $75 one-time minimum purchase, and subscription rendering at **$0.20 per rendered minute** with a $39 monthly minimum. A final 100-second VistaFlow listing video therefore represents 1.667 rendered minutes before rounding or provider-specific allowances.

Source: https://shotstack.io/pricing/
